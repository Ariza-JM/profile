"I’m Juan Manuel, a Doctor in Epidemiology with a deep passion for archaeology and a focus on understanding how studying past epidemics can help us prevent future health crises."

Currently, I am a lecturer at the Nantes School of Veterinary Medicine in France and the director of the "One Health Emerge" Master’s program at the University of Nantes. My academic and professional journey has always been driven by a fascination with the past, particularly archaeology. In pursuit of a new direction in my scientific and pedagogical career, I will begin a Master’s degree in Archaeology, with a special focus on paleopathology. My ultimate goal is to develop a research line in "One Health archaeology," bridging zooarchaeology and archaeothanatology to explore ancient health dynamics.

As part of this professional transition, I am actively engaging in archaeological scientific activities, including excavations and laboratory work, through short internships. These experiences are helping me gain a deeper understanding of the field and prepare for the interdisciplinary research I aim to pursue.

In my free time, I enjoy reading and climbing....

If you’re interested in my past work, feel free to check out my research papers[papers](assets/tochange.pdf ) 

And stay tuned—new archaeology papers will be coming soon!
