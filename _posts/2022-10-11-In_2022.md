"I’m Juan Manuel, a Doctor of Epidemiology. My current research focuses on exploring how past health crises can help us prevent future emerging epidemics"

Currently, I am a lecturer at the Nantes School of Veterinary Medicine in France and the director of the "One Health Emerge" Master’s, a joint program with Nantes University. My academic and professional journey has always been driven by a fascination with the past. In pursuit of a new direction in my scientific and pedagogical career, I will begin a Master’s degree in Archaeology, with a special focus on paleoepidemiology. My ultimate goal is to develop a research line in 'One Health archaeology', bridging veterinary science, food systems, zooarchaeology, paleopthaology, and archaeothanatology to explore ancient health dynamics.

As part of this professional transition, I am actively engaging in archaeological scientific activities, including excavations and laboratory work, through short internships. These experiences are helping me gain a deeper understanding of the field and prepare for the interdisciplinary research I aim to pursue.

If you’re interested in my past work, feel free to check out my research papers

And stay tuned—new archaeology papers will be coming soon!
